# TheBillQuest

### Bill management made simpler.

**TheBillQuest** is an early-stage fintech product I’m building to explore a simpler way for consumers to organize recurring bills, track upcoming payments, and reduce the friction of managing bills across multiple providers.

> This repository is a public product showcase. The production source code and sensitive infrastructure remain private.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Consumers often manage recurring bills across multiple provider websites, due dates, payment methods, and reminders.

This creates several problems:

- Bills are scattered across different platforms
- Due dates are easy to lose track of
- Users lack one clear view of upcoming obligations
- Managing multiple accounts creates unnecessary friction
- Existing tools often focus on tracking spending after the fact rather than simplifying the bill-management workflow

---

## 💡 Product Vision

TheBillQuest is being designed as a centralized bill-management experience where users can:

- Organize recurring bills in one place
- Track upcoming due dates
- Receive payment reminders
- Maintain a clearer view of bill activity

### Future roadmap

The longer-term product direction includes exploring:

- Connected payment experiences
- On-time payment rewards
- Expanded recurring-bill categories
- Personalized financial insights

These capabilities are part of the product roadmap and are not represented here as fully launched features.

---

## 🔎 Customer Validation

Before expanding development, I gathered feedback from approximately **350–400 prospective users across multiple U.S. states**.

The research focused on:

- Current bill-payment habits
- Pain points with managing multiple bills
- Interest in centralized bill management
- Reminder preferences
- Feature priorities
- Early demand signals

Customer feedback has been used to guide product prioritization and rollout decisions.
